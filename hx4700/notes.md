# hx4700 frame: notes per pass

Final: `final/hx4700-framed-template.svg` (generated by `gen_hx4700.py`, same as `pass7/hx4700.svg`).

## Method and conventions

- Reference: `frames-ref/HP_iPAQ_hx4700_-_Front.jpg` (Beamish4, CC BY-SA 4.0). EXIF orientation 6. `ref/upright.png` is the image turned upright, and only that was used to look at. Nothing from the photo is in the SVG.
- `tools/rectify.py` maps the four corners of the lit-glass area (the lighter LCD area inside the black border, located with line fits in `tools/edges.py`) onto an upright 3:4 rectangle at x 1100..2876, y 1650..4018 (`ref/rect.png`). All measurements are in those "photo px". Scale: 525/1776 = 0.29561 units per px.
- In the photo the unlit LCD area is 1.9 % taller than 3:4, and the rectification squeezes that out. Cross-check: the body is 77 mm wide (published 131 x 77 mm). That gives 61.4 mm for the glass width against about 61 mm for a 4 inch 3:4 panel. The drawn body's ratio is 1.71 against 1.70 for the published size.
- Parallax: the glass sits about 20 px right of the face-plane centre (the camera stood to the right; the right side wall shows). The drawing is a front view, so face-plane features are measured from the face centre (photo x 1966) and drawn from the glass centre (1988). The comparison crops shift the photo by 22 px to match.
- Glass: `<rect id="screen">` x=137.5 y=244.75 525x700, as asked. The body top lands at y 26.9 units. That is enough for the shadow (blur 12, dy 10): the top row of the canvas has alpha 0. Canvas 366x556 CSS px, viewBox `-0.3125 -0.25 800.625 1216.25`, transparent background, shadow in its own `#shadow` group.
- Rendering: `qlmanage -t -s 1000|2000` scales the SVG to the full width and crops it to a square, so it never shows the lower part of this tall canvas. Those PNGs are kept in each pass directory, but all comparisons use headless Chrome (`tools/render.sh`, 4x). `tools/compare.py` writes `sbs-<region>.png`: the rectified photo on the left and the drawing on the right, cut from the same region and scaled to the same size.

## Pass 1

Saw: the whole face was mid-grey and too light, with a visibly repeating dot-lattice texture (a 48 px pattern tile). The bottom recess wall was barely visible, while in the photo it is a bright grainy band. The pad had a strong dark inner rim and a lattice texture. The speaker slots were thin white lines with no dark slit. The labels under the glyphs were too visible.
Changed: replaced the tile pattern with an feTurbulence grain filter (no repeat). Darkened the face. Brightened the bottom wall and gave grain to the lit walls only. Made the pad rim lighter. Made the slots wider and dark, with the highlight on their right. Made the labels fainter.

## Pass 2

Saw (top-left, top-right, badge, lower-right, side-left): the grain looked like blotchy worms, not fine sparkle. In the photo the left recess wall is grainy too, only a little darker than the face. The body's top-left corner was too round, and the front-edge highlight followed the corner down the side instead of ending at it. The top highlight was a bright line across the whole cap (in the photo it is faint over the cap and bright only at the left corner). The LEDs were too small and too dark. The cap's lower corners needed a larger radius (about 50). The badge disc had a full white ring, while the photo shows a lit crescent only on the right. The p descender was short and the letters thin. The face's bottom corners in the photo are chamfered (a straight slope from the side to the black panel), not big round corners. The black panel needed a bottom lip.
Changed: tighter top corners, side inset 8, chamfered bottom corners. A faint top edge plus a bright short highlight at the top-left corner. Bigger and lighter LEDs. Cap corner radius 50. Finer, higher-contrast grain. Grain on all walls. Badge with a crescent, bolder letters and a longer descender. Black lip under the panel. Lighter left edge band, darker right one.

## Pass 3

Saw: the overall view was close. The lower-left crop showed the bottom-left corner still too low and too round. The black panel's top corner was too sharp, and its edge sat about 20 px too far out. The "iPAQ" was a touch too white and heavy. The plate grain made the chrome look grey and noisy.
Changed: body and face bottom profile re-measured from the crop (chamfer (1062,4668)..(884,4733)). Panel half-width 650 at y 4220 (re-measured: the left and right edges are symmetric about 1968), corner radius 58. "iPAQ" colour #dedede. Plate grain at 0.12.

## Pass 4 and 5

Saw (pad-buttons): the pad, its dots and the glyph positions match within a few px. The face below the screen was lighter and grainier than in the photo, which is a dark charcoal there.
Changed: darker vertical face gradient (lower stops #353336 and #2f2d30), grain opacity 0.62.

## Pass 6

Saw (silhouette, top-right): the proportions and features match. In the photo the rounded top band over the grey top-right corner is a lighter grey band. The cap's top band in the drawing was too red and too light.
Changed: a lighter grey band between the silhouette and the face along the top (the cap covers it where it is black). The cap band tint is down to 0.22.

## Pass 7 (final)

Saw (top): the cap, LEDs, wireless mark, badge, speaker and corner shapes all line up with the photo. The remaining differences are listed in the final report.
